The 10 Commandments of Privacy for Being a Good Developer ES

We all know what privacy is until we have to define it. We all know what respecting privacy means until we actually have to do it. And we all know how to manage our app’s data… until the fine arrives.

In this talk, I try to simplify what privacy should mean for developers and share 10 commandments so that the next time you have to wrestle with it, you won’t get caught with your pants down.

I gave this talk at Commit Conf 2026.

You Would NOT Do It Better ES

Software engineers and marketing people are natural enemies. Just like software engineers and HR. Or software engineers and salespeople. Or software engineers and other software engineers… Damn engineers, you ruined engineering! Or, at least, that’s been my experience after a year in marketing. Life takes many turns, and I, an engineer in love with development, spent my last working year deeply immersed in a marketing team doing what I thought was going to be a simple job. Because I could do it better than them. But it’s not that easy, and today I’m going to explain why.

I gave this talk at T3chFest 2026.

Ethics, Data, Experiences - Bridging the gap between innovation and responsibility EN

We claim that there is a gap between technical curiosity and ethical boundaries. Technically and intellectually, using statistical models to infer human behaviour poses interesting challenges. They can provide insightful inferences, and the fast-paced development leads to rapid increase in use-cases.

Relevance is not a Thing but a Perception EN

When talking about relevance regarding search, it often sounds like it is a thing, something that can be touched and seen. Nevertheless, that is not the case. What do I mean by that? In this talk, I will provide some examples of how relevance is often merely seen as a score when it can be, in fact, an engaging relationship where the user and the search UI connect in aesthetic and enjoyable ways. I will present numerous examples of innovative search experiences that challenge prevailing schemas and structures and lead instead to elements of motion and correlated visual action that allows us to perceive the beauty of relevancy on a different level. Because relevance is a matter of perception.
