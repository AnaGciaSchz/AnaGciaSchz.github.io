If you've ever thought about taking your career in a different direction and weren't sure it was worth it, this conversation might help.

I recently had the honor of being interviewed by Natalia De Pablo García for Codemotion Magazine, and today you can finally enjoy the result ☺️

We talked about career paths that aren't linear: about changing roles, the doubts that come up along the way, questioning what you're doing... About how stepping off the "expected" path isn't a step backward, but another way of building a career, because we're always learning something that ends up paying off.

🔗 You can read the full interview [here](https://www.codemotion.com/magazine/es/dev-life-es/historias/ana-garcia-no-puedes-ser-puente-entre-cosas-que-no-conoces/).

Thank you so much, Natalia, for the opportunity to share my story so it can help someone who needs it, and for all the work and time you put into telling it well. It was a real pleasure ^^
