Here I share the material I prepare for my talks, so you can download it and read it whenever you like. For now these resources are only available in Spanish.

<h3 id="charlame">Chárlame como a una de tus chicas asturianas <span class="lang-tag">ES</span></h3>

Most speakers gave their first talk feeling they weren't ready. I know many people want to try being a speaker, even just once, but feel they lack the tools. That's what this talk is about 😉

This is an adapted version to read at your own pace, with what has worked for me: practical tips, techniques to manage your nerves and details you only learn by speaking in public many times. The comments in red are what I would say out loud during the live talk.

At the end you'll find a feedback form. I want to keep polishing this talk until it becomes THE definitive talk about giving talks! 👀
