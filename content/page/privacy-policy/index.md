This website is my personal space, and I care about privacy as much online as I do in my work. Here's a plain-language summary of what happens with your data when you visit.

<h3>Comments</h3>

Comments are powered by [Cusdis](https://cusdis.com/), a privacy-first commenting system. You can leave a comment with just a nickname; adding an email is optional and the information will only be used to notify me of your comment and be public when the comment is published. Cusdis does not track visitors or build advertising profiles.

<h3>Analytics and cookies</h3>

I don't use any analytics or advertising trackers on this site, and I don't set any cookies of my own.

<h3>Embedded content</h3>

Some posts and pages embed YouTube videos. If you play one, YouTube (Google) may set its own cookies and collect data according to [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Links to external sites (LinkedIn, GitHub, event pages, etc.) are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

<h3>Hosting</h3>

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address as part of serving the site, per [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

<h3>Questions?</h3>

If you have any questions about this policy, feel free to reach out via [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anagciaschz/).
